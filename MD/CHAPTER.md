## Outreach & Messaging

### Chapter Elevator Pitch (use in emails, social, conversations)
> Think Big St. Louis is a BNI chapter of business professionals in the Kirkwood/St. Louis area
> who meet every Thursday at 11:30 AM to pass qualified referrals to each other.
> With only one member per profession, there's zero internal competition —
> just a committed group of business owners helping each other grow.

### Key Value Props (use in outreach copy)
1. **One seat per profession** — lock out your competition in the room
2. **Structured referrals** — not just networking, actual business passed every week
3. **$38,100 average annual revenue** generated per BNI member
4. **50 meetings/year** — consistent, accountable, proven format
5. **Local St. Louis network** — Kirkwood, MO — real community connections
6. **Global reach** — 355,000+ members in 76 countries through BNI Connect

### Objection Handling
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I tried networking and it didn't work" | BNI is structured referral passing, not casual mingling — members are accountable to give referrals |
| "I don't have time" | One meeting per week, 11:30–1 PM — and the ROI is $38K avg per member |
| "Is my category open?" | Check thinkbig.webtek.ai — one seat per profession, first come first served |
| "I'm not sure BNI is for me" | Guests visit twice free — no commitment, just come see it |

## Email Outreach Sequences

### Sequence 1 — Cold Outreach (Scraped / CSV Prospects)

**Email 1 — Day 0 — Intro**
- Subject: `[First Name], there's an open [Profession] seat in Kirkwood`
- Tone: Personal, curious, short. About them.
- CTA: Visit thinkbig.webtek.ai to learn more

**Email 2 — Day 4 — Value**
- Subject: `How [First Name]'s business could get referrals every week`
- Tone: Specific benefits. BNI stats. One ask.
- CTA: Register to visit a Thursday meeting

**Email 3 — Day 8 — Soft close**
- Subject: `Last thought — your Thursday seat at Think Big, [First Name]`
- Tone: Short, direct, easy out.
- CTA: Register or reply to opt out

### Sequence 2 — Visitor Follow-up (Already registered)
*(Handled by automated cron — see TECH-STACK.md)*
- Day 0: Welcome SMS + Email
- Day 1 after visit: SMS follow-up
- Day 7 after visit: SMS nudge
- Day 14 after visit: Email application reminder
